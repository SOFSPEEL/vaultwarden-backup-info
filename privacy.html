Vaultwarden Backup Privacy Policy

Updated October 3, 2026.

Vaultwarden Backup is a personal utility operated by the owner of this OAuth application, using rclone on the owner's Mac.

Google Drive access

The app requests the drive.file permission to create and manage files created by or explicitly authorized for this app. It uses file contents and metadata to upload, list, download, and maintain encrypted backup archives.

Storage and use

Backup archives are encrypted locally with age before upload to Google Drive. The decryption key remains separate from the uploaded archives. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the owner's Mac. Workflow logs may contain backup filenames and operational status.

Google Drive data is used only for the owner's backup and restore operations. This utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Retention and control

The backup process retains seven daily, four weekly, and three monthly backup periods. Superseded backup files are moved to Drive's trash through rclone. The owner can remove backups in Google Drive and revoke the app's access in Google Account settings.

This policy describes this backup utility; Google's services are subject to Google's own policies.
